## Hotel Santa Fe Puerto Escondido &nbsp;

### Introduction
Set along the shores of Zicatela Beach, Hotel Santa Fe Puerto Escondido is a charming beachfront retreat that captures the relaxed spirit and cultural richness of Oaxaca’s Pacific coast. Combining traditional Mexican architecture with tropical gardens, inviting swimming pools, and warm hospitality, the hotel offers a laid-back escape surrounded by lush greenery and the sound of the ocean. Its distinctive style, featuring regional materials and handcrafted details, creates an authentic sense of place, making it an appealing choice for travellers looking to experience the natural beauty and cultural character of Puerto Escondido.

![](/images/hotels/santafe/santafe1.jpg)

### Location
Located on Avenida del Morro in the Zicatela area of Puerto Escondido, Hotel Santa Fe sits directly opposite Zicatela Beach, one of Mexico’s best-known surfing destinations. Famous for its powerful waves, expansive shoreline, and vibrant surf culture, the beach offers a spectacular Pacific setting. Nearby, Puerto Escondido’s different neighbourhoods reveal a more relaxed side of coastal Mexico, with seafood restaurants, cafés, local markets, and laid-back beach communities. The hotel also provides a convenient base for discovering the region’s quieter beaches, coastal landscapes, and the traditions of Oaxaca.

![](/images/hotels/santafe/santafe2.jpg)

### What to Do
Spend leisurely days beside the hotel’s swimming pools, surrounded by tropical vegetation, or head across to Zicatela Beach to watch surfers and admire the Pacific sunsets. Explore Puerto Escondido’s different beach neighbourhoods, browse local shops, and discover the cafés and restaurants that give the destination its relaxed character. Nature lovers can arrange excursions to explore the surrounding coastline, lagoons, and local wildlife, while those interested in regional culture can discover the culinary traditions and artisan heritage of Oaxaca. In the evening, return to the hotel for a relaxed meal beneath the traditional palapa roof or enjoy a drink amid the tropical surroundings.

![](/images/hotels/santafe/santafe3.jpg)

### Why We Recommend This Hotel
A beachfront setting – Located directly opposite Zicatela Beach, the hotel offers easy access to the Pacific shoreline, surf culture, and spectacular sunsets. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Authentic Mexican character – Traditional architecture, regional materials, and decorative details create a distinctive atmosphere rooted in the character of coastal Oaxaca. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Tropical surroundings – Lush gardens and inviting swimming pools provide a peaceful retreat between beach outings and local adventures. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
A taste of Oaxaca – The hotel’s restaurant celebrates Mexican and regional flavours, offering guests an opportunity to discover the culinary traditions of Oaxaca. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
A relaxed coastal atmosphere – The combination of tropical gardens, traditional design, and easy beach access makes the hotel well suited to travellers seeking a laid-back seaside escape. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
A base for exploration – Its location makes it easy to discover Puerto Escondido’s beaches, local food scene, and surrounding natural landscapes. &nbsp;  

### Dining Options
Hotel Santa Fe Restaurant serves Mexican and regional cuisine in a traditional palapa setting, where tropical surroundings contribute to the relaxed dining atmosphere. Guests can discover flavours inspired by Oaxaca alongside dishes that celebrate the region’s fresh ingredients and coastal culinary traditions. The restaurant provides an inviting setting for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, while the poolside area offers a more casual place to unwind with refreshments. Beyond the hotel, Puerto Escondido’s dining scene features fresh seafood, traditional Oaxacan specialities, and relaxed beachfront restaurants, offering plenty of opportunities to explore the region’s distinctive flavours.
