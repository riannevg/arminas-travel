## Almare, a Luxury Collection Resort, Isla Mujeres, Adult All-Inclusive &nbsp;

### Introduction  
Nestled along the turquoise shores of Isla Mujeres, **Almare, a Luxury Collection Resort, Isla Mujeres, Adult All-Inclusive** is an intimate adults-only sanctuary where contemporary luxury meets the island's natural beauty. Designed to foster a deep connection with its surroundings, the resort seamlessly blends sophisticated architecture, locally inspired design, and personalised service to create an atmosphere of tranquillity and exclusivity. Every experience is thoughtfully curated, inviting guests to slow down, reconnect, and embrace the relaxed rhythm of island life. 

![](/images/hotels/almare/almare2.jpeg)

### Location  
Located on the peaceful eastern coast of Isla Mujeres, **Almare, a Luxury Collection Resort, Isla Mujeres, Adult All-Inclusive** offers a secluded beachfront setting just a short ferry ride from Cancún. Surrounded by crystal-clear Caribbean waters and pristine white-sand beaches, the resort provides the perfect balance between serenity and accessibility. Guests can easily explore the colourful streets of Isla Mujeres, discover the island's natural wonders, or simply unwind while taking in panoramic ocean views. 

![](/images/hotels/almare/almare3.jpeg)

### What to Do  
Whether you prefer relaxation or exploration, **Almare, a Luxury Collection Resort, Isla Mujeres, Adult All-Inclusive** offers experiences inspired by the destination's culture and natural beauty. Begin the day with sunrise yoga overlooking the Caribbean, rejuvenate at Alma Spa with treatments rooted in Mayan wellness traditions, or discover the island through guided walks and water activities. Culinary enthusiasts can participate in immersive experiences such as cacao workshops, mezcal tastings, and demonstrations of traditional Yucatecan recipes, while those seeking adventure can explore nearby coral reefs, snorkel in crystal-clear waters, or charter a private catamaran to experience the beauty of the Mexican Caribbean.

### Dining Options  
Dining at **Almare, a Luxury Collection Resort, Isla Mujeres, Adult All-Inclusive** is an immersive journey through the flavours of the Mexican Caribbean. **BOGA** showcases authentic Mayan and Mexican cuisine crafted with seasonal local ingredients, while **Seasalt** pairs spectacular 360-degree ocean views with a refined fusion of Mediterranean and Mexican gastronomy. Guests can enjoy artisanal coffee and locally sourced cacao at **Botanik**, healthy grab-and-go dishes at **AlPaso**, fresh seafood and tropical cocktails at **AlVento**, and handcrafted tiki-inspired drinks at **The Breeze** pool bar. Throughout the resort, culinary pop-ups, mezcal tastings, cacao experiences, and private beachfront dining create memorable moments that celebrate the region's rich gastronomic traditions.
