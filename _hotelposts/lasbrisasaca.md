## Las Brisas Acapulco &nbsp;

### Introduction  
Perched on the lush hills overlooking the legendary Bay of Acapulco, Las Brisas Acapulco is an iconic Mexican retreat celebrated for its spectacular panoramic views, romantic atmosphere, and distinctive style of hospitality. Set within tropical gardens, this exclusive resort offers a collection of private casitas, many featuring their own swimming pools and spacious terraces overlooking the Pacific coastline. From the signature pink margarita offered upon arrival to the hotel's iconic pink-and-white jeeps, every detail contributes to a memorable experience that combines timeless glamour with the relaxed spirit of Acapulco.

![](/images/hotels/lasbrisasaca/lasbrisasaca1.jpg)

### Location  
Located along the scenic Carretera Escénica in the hills above Acapulco, Las Brisas Acapulco enjoys an elevated position with sweeping views of the bay and the Pacific Ocean. Its tranquil setting provides a welcome retreat from the city's lively beaches and entertainment districts, while remaining within easy reach of Acapulco's principal attractions. Guests can explore the historic centre, discover the famous cliff divers of La Quebrada, visit the traditional neighbourhoods, or enjoy the restaurants and nightlife that have made Acapulco one of Mexico's most celebrated coastal destinations.

![](/images/hotels/lasbrisasaca/lasbrisasaca2.jpg)

### What to Do  
Days at Las Brisas Acapulco can be spent enjoying the privacy of your own terrace and swimming pool, surrounded by tropical vegetation and spectacular ocean views. Guests can also visit La Concha Beach Club, an exclusive seaside retreat featuring swimming pools and a bar, ideal for a relaxing afternoon by the water. For a memorable excursion, arrange a private yacht experience to witness the legendary cliff divers of La Quebrada as they plunge into the Pacific from dramatic rocky heights. The hotel's iconic jeeps also provide a distinctive way to explore the surrounding area, while Acapulco's beaches, cultural attractions, and vibrant dining scene offer plenty of opportunities to discover the destination beyond the resort.

![](/images/hotels/lasbrisasaca/lasbrisasaca3.jpg)

### Dining Options  
Dining at Las Brisas Acapulco combines memorable views with a variety of culinary experiences. Bellavista Restaurant offers an elegant setting for enjoying a meal overlooking the bay, while Bellavista Bar provides a relaxed atmosphere for drinks and socialising. At La Concha Restaurant, guests can enjoy a seaside dining experience at the hotel's exclusive beach club, surrounded by the beauty of the Pacific coast. Sunset Bar offers another inviting setting for cocktails and evening relaxation. For a more private experience, guests can also enjoy meals on the terrace of their own casita, where the panoramic views and tranquil surroundings make dining an especially memorable part of the stay.
