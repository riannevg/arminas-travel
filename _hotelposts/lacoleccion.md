## La Colección Residences &nbsp;

### Introduction
La Colección Residences offers an exclusive private-island retreat on Isla Mujeres, where the comfort and privacy of a luxury home meet the personalised hospitality and services of a luxury hotel. Set directly on the beach overlooking the turquoise waters of the Caribbean Sea, these sophisticated residences combine contemporary architecture with natural materials, tropical landscaping, and interiors inspired by the island’s marine environment. Developed by Grupo Artila and designed by Artigas Arquitectos, the collection comprises three distinctive residences: Casa Ceiba, Casa Coral, and Casa Azul, each with its own design identity. With expansive living spaces, private outdoor areas, and dedicated staff, La Colección Residences is designed for travellers seeking an exceptional and highly personalised Caribbean escape.

![](/images/hotels/lacoleccion/lacoleccion2.jpg)

### Location
Situated on Isla Mujeres, just approximately 20 minutes from Cancún by sea, La Colección Residences enjoys a privileged beachfront setting surrounded by the spectacular turquoise waters of the Caribbean. The island offers a more intimate and relaxed atmosphere than mainland Cancún, with beautiful beaches, charming local streets, and opportunities to discover its marine life and coastal scenery. Guests arrive via a private transfer from Cancún International Airport to the marina, followed by a yacht journey to the island, creating a memorable beginning to the stay. The residences' exclusive pier also provides convenient access for private boat experiences and exploring the surrounding coastline.

![](/images/hotels/lacoleccion/lacoleccion3.jpg)

### What to Do
Spend leisurely days enjoying direct beach access, swimming in the Caribbean, or relaxing beside your residence's heated swimming pool and jacuzzi. Take advantage of the kayaks and paddleboards to explore the calm surrounding waters, or discover Isla Mujeres by bicycle at your own pace. For a more exclusive experience, arrange a private boat charter to explore the coastline and nearby waters. The concierge can also organise island excursions and bespoke activities tailored to individual preferences. Guests looking for enriching experiences can arrange cooking and ceramics classes, mixology sessions, live music, or wellness activities such as massages, yoga, meditation, and sound therapy. Families can also request creative workshops for children, including piñata-making, ceramics, and yoga.

### Why We Recommend This Hotel
An exclusive private-residence concept – The collection combines the space and privacy of a luxury home with dedicated hospitality services, making it particularly appealing for families and groups travelling together. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Three distinctive residences – Casa Ceiba, Casa Coral, and Casa Azul each have an individual design identity inspired by nature, marine life, or the turquoise Caribbean Sea. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Spacious accommodation – Each residence features six bedrooms, generous living areas, a family room, a fully equipped kitchen, and an elevator, providing ample space for shared stays while preserving personal privacy. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Private outdoor living – Gardens, terraces, heated swimming pools, jacuzzis, and direct beach access create a seamless connection between indoor comfort and the Caribbean setting. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Dedicated personalised service – A concierge, executive chef, cook, and waiter-bartender help create a tailored stay, with meals served at pre-confirmed times and daytime assistance available on the beach. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
A private yacht arrival – The journey from Cancún by private transfer and yacht adds an exclusive dimension to the arrival experience. &nbsp;  
&nbsp;  
Experiences tailored to your stay – From private boat charters and live music to cooking classes, mixology, and wellness treatments, a variety of additional experiences can be arranged in advance. &nbsp;

### Dining Options
La Colección Residences offers a private dining experience centred around each residence, rather than a conventional hotel restaurant. An executive chef, cook, and waiter-bartender provide personalised culinary service, with breakfast, lunch, and dinner included at pre-confirmed times. Guests can enjoy meals in the elegant indoor dining room, accompanied by a private bar, or dine outdoors on the terrace overlooking the garden and Caribbean Sea. The outdoor areas feature a grill bar and a wood-fired pizza oven, creating opportunities for relaxed al fresco meals and convivial evenings with family and friends. Soft drinks and national beers are also included, while additional experiences such as private yacht outings and special mixology sessions can be arranged on request.
