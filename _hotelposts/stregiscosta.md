## The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancún &nbsp;

### Introduction  
Set along the pristine shores of the Mexican Caribbean, **The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancún** is a sophisticated beachfront sanctuary where timeless elegance meets contemporary luxury. Inspired by the natural beauty and rich heritage of Quintana Roo, the resort offers an elevated escape defined by impeccable service, refined design, and the legendary St. Regis Butler Service. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to create unforgettable moments, from sunrise over the turquoise sea to the resort's signature evening champagne sabrage ritual. 

![](/images/hotels/stregiscosta/stregiscosta2.jpeg)

### Location  
Nestled in the exclusive enclave of Costa Mujeres, just north of Cancún, **The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancún** enjoys a privileged setting surrounded by powder-soft white beaches, crystal-clear Caribbean waters, and protected natural landscapes. The resort is only a short drive from Cancún International Airport while remaining close to attractions such as Isla Contoy, the Mesoamerican Reef, Isla Mujeres, and the archaeological site of El Meco, making it the perfect base for both relaxation and exploration. 

![](/images/hotels/stregiscosta/stregiscosta3.jpeg)

### What to Do  
Whether you seek adventure or tranquillity, **The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancún** offers experiences tailored to every traveller. Relax beside the oceanfront infinity pool, indulge in ancestral Mayan-inspired treatments at The St. Regis Spa, or allow the renowned Butler Service to curate bespoke moments throughout your stay. Venture beyond the resort to snorkel the vibrant Mesoamerican Reef, kayak through peaceful mangroves, discover the wildlife of Isla Contoy, or explore the nearby Mayan ruins of El Meco. As evening falls, join the iconic St. Regis champagne sabrage ceremony before enjoying handcrafted cocktails overlooking the Caribbean Sea. 

![](/images/hotels/stregiscosta/stregiscosta4.jpeg)

### Dining Options  
Dining at **The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancún** is a celebration of the flavours and traditions of the Mexican Caribbean. **Costa Coral** presents a refined interpretation of regional cuisine inspired by ancestral fishing traditions and the biodiversity of the milpa, while **Fish Market** showcases fresh seafood, signature ceviches, and dishes designed for sharing. At **Palmare Grill**, guests can enjoy elevated beachside cuisine inspired by Mexico's vibrant street food culture, while **Olivia** offers premium Mexican coffees and artisanal pastries throughout the day. The experience is completed at **The St. Regis Bar**, home to creative mixology, The Tequila Library, and the brand's iconic evening rituals, making every meal and cocktail an unforgettable part of the stay.
